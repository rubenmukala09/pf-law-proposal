Privacy policy

What we collect through this website, why, and the choices you have.

Draft for the firm’s counsel to review before launch.

What we collect

When you request a consultation, we collect the details you choose to give us: your name, phone number, email address, the type of matter, and any note you add. When you browse the site, standard technical information such as your browser type, pages visited and approximate location may be recorded by our analytics tools.

How we use it

  • To respond to your request and schedule a consultation.
  • To check for conflicts of interest before we can discuss your matter.
  • To understand how the website is used so we can improve it.

We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers who help us run the website, scheduling and client intake, under agreements that limit their use of it, or when the law requires it.

Keep it general

Information you send before the firm agrees to represent you is not protected the same way as communications with your lawyer. Please keep early messages general and do not include confidential details.

Cookies and analytics

The site may use cookies and similar tools for basic analytics. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the site will still work.

Your choices

To ask what information we hold about you, or to ask us to correct or delete it, email info@pf-lawllc.com or call (404) 355-9400.

Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page with a new effective date.
