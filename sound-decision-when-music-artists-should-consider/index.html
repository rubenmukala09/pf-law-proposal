The sound decision: When music artists should consider hiring an attorney

When should a music artist hire an attorney? Signs to watch for, what to review before signing recording or publishing deals, and how copyright, trademark, licensing, and business formation protect a career.

By The Law Offices of Precious Felder, LLC

Every music artist dreams of writing the next hit, signing the right deal, and building a lasting career. But the music industry is filled with contracts, rights, royalties, and negotiations that can make or break an artist’s future. Knowing when to hire an Atlanta music industry attorney is one of the most important business decisions any artist will make. At The Law Offices of Precious Felder, LLC, we represent Atlanta musicians, producers, songwriters, and labels at every stage of their careers.

Signs you need an Atlanta music industry attorney

If you have been offered a recording, publishing, or management agreement, or if someone is using your music without permission, it is time to call an Atlanta music industry attorney. Other signs include disputes over royalty payments, songwriting credits, producer splits, or sample clearances.

Before you sign: Recording and publishing contracts

Never sign a recording or publishing contract without review by an experienced Atlanta music industry attorney. These contracts typically contain clauses on advances, recoupment, ownership of masters, term length, options, territory, and net versus gross royalties. One unfavorable clause can cost you hundreds of thousands of dollars over a career.

Copyright registration for musicians

Every original song is a copyrightable asset. Our Atlanta copyright registration attorneys file with the U.S. Copyright Office so your work is protected from day one. Registered copyrights unlock statutory damages, attorney’s fees, and stronger enforcement rights against infringers.

Trademark your artist name and brand

Your stage name, group name, and logo are brand assets. An Atlanta trademark registration attorney can federally register these marks with the USPTO, giving you nationwide protection against copycats and bad-faith registrants.

Licensing, sync deals, and royalty disputes

An Atlanta music attorney negotiates sync licenses for film, TV, advertising, and streaming, and resolves royalty disputes with distributors, publishers, and co-writers. Strong paperwork today prevents lawsuits tomorrow.

Business formation for Atlanta musicians

Professional artists should operate through an LLC or S-Corp for liability protection and tax efficiency. Our Atlanta business formation attorneys help artists structure publishing companies, production entities, and record labels the right way from day one.

This article is general information about Georgia law as of its publication date, not legal advice. For advice about your situation, book a consultation or call (404) 355-9400.
