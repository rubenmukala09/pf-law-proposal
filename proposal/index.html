Design proposal

A faster, clearer, more trustworthy website.

A full redesign of pf-lawllc.com using the firm’s own content, photography and URLs, measured against the current site.

What changed.

Privacy policy, legal disclaimer and accessibility pages
Drafted and linked in every footer, plus a no-attorney-client-relationship line next to the form.
Three competing pop-ups
Replaced by one sticky bar with Call and Book a consultation. No chat auto-open, no modal.
Gold buttons at 2.07:1 contrast
Every action is navy on white or light navy on dark, at 7:1 contrast or better.
Spliced, cut-off testimonials and typos
Full reviews restored from the testimonial page; copy proofread on every page.
No H1 on inner pages, three on the home page
Exactly one H1 per page, with landmarks and a working skip link.
6.6 MB home page, 58 stylesheets, ~35 scripts
Static HTML, AVIF/WebP photos at the size shown, almost no JavaScript.
Weak schema, "christinaccf" as author
LegalService, Person, Service, FAQPage, BlogPosting and BreadcrumbList structured data.
Three domains (pf-lawllc, famlegalatl, entlegalatl)
Family Law and Entertainment Law become sections of one site, one phone number, one brand.
Stale homepage clone and WooCommerce pages indexed
Old URLs redirect to their new homes; same practice-area URLs kept for rankings.
“Top-Rated”, “Best”, open-ended “LIMITED TIME”
Removed from firm-voice copy; results and reviews carry disclaimers.
English only
Every page in seven languages: English, Spanish, Chinese, Tagalog, Vietnamese, Arabic (right to left) and French, the languages most spoken in US homes. Each translated page says the English version controls.
No client or staff tools
Two working demos: a staff CRM with the lead pipeline, matters, calendar, tasks and billing, and a client portal with case progress, documents, messages and statements. Requests sent from the contact form show up in the CRM.
Questions after hours go unanswered
The PF Assistant answers on every page in seven languages and tells visitors where a request they sent stands. It says up front that it is an AI and that chatting creates no attorney-client relationship. It never gives legal advice, and it puts anyone in danger in front of 911 and the Georgia and national hotlines first.
Intake invites case stories before any conflict check
Conflict-first intake (Georgia Rule 1.18): the form and the assistant ask only for a name, the other party and the type of matter. Details typed anyway are refused and hidden in the chat, and website notes stay embargoed in the CRM until the conflict check clears.
No way to search the site
One search across practice areas, attorneys, articles and answers, in every language, from the menu, the home page or Ctrl/Cmd + K.

Before launch.

  1. Firm’s counsel reviews the privacy policy, legal disclaimer, results wording and the bios.
  2. Attorney confirms FAQ wording on Georgia divorce grounds (“irretrievably broken”) and separate maintenance.
  3. Confirm office days and hours, and which job openings are current.
  4. Connect the consultation form to the firm’s intake system (Intaker or MyCase).
  5. Point famlegalatl.com and entlegalatl.com at the new division pages with 301 redirects.
  6. A qualified legal translator reviews each language; the translation notes list the terms to confirm first.
  7. Connect the CRM and portal to the firm’s practice-management system (MyCase) before any real client data goes in. The demos keep sample data in the browser only.
  8. Connect the assistants to Claude through a server-side proxy (the API key never reaches the browser), and have counsel review their answer wording and guardrails.
  9. A Georgia lawyer approves every scripted assistant answer, knowledge entry and system prompt before launch (Rule 5.3), and a native-speaker lawyer reviews the new warning strings in each language.
  10. The AI vendor contract forbids training on visitor data and sets retention and deletion terms; transcripts stay out of analytics and remarketing.
  11. Before real payments: retainers and unearned fees go to the trust/IOLTA account, fees and chargebacks come from operating (Rule 1.15), with authenticated access, encryption and audit logs on the portal.
  12. Remove the proposal banner and the noindex tag, then submit the sitemap in Search Console.

Built as a static Astro site. It can keep reading content from the firm’s existing WordPress, so staff edit where they already do.
