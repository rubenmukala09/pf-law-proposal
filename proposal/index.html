Design proposal

A faster, clearer, more trustworthy website.

A full redesign of pf-lawllc.com using the firm’s own content, photography and URLs, measured against the current site.

Measured, not guessed.

Lighthouse lab testCurrent siteThis proposal
Performance score (mobile)30100
Largest Contentful Paint24.7 s1.7 s
Total Blocking Time6,520 ms0 ms
Cumulative Layout Shift (desktop)0.540
Accessibility score93100
Best practices score6978*
Homepage weight6.6 MB130 KB
Requests on the homepage1599

Lighthouse 13.5, simulated mid-range phone on slow 4G, run 18 and 19 Oct 2026. Lab numbers, not field data. *The proposal was tested over plain HTTP on a local machine, which costs two best-practice checks that pass once it is served over HTTPS.

What changed.

Privacy policy, legal disclaimer and accessibility pages
Drafted and linked in every footer, plus a no-attorney-client-relationship line next to the form.
Three competing pop-ups
Replaced by one sticky bar with Call and Book a consultation. No chat auto-open, no modal.
Gold buttons at 2.07:1 contrast
Every action is navy on white or light navy on dark, at 7:1 contrast or better.
Spliced, cut-off testimonials and typos
Full reviews restored from the testimonial page; copy proofread on every page.
No H1 on inner pages, three on the home page
Exactly one H1 per page, with landmarks and a working skip link.
6.6 MB home page, 58 stylesheets, ~35 scripts
Static HTML, AVIF/WebP photos at the size shown, almost no JavaScript.
Weak schema, "christinaccf" as author
LegalService, Person, Service, FAQPage, BlogPosting and BreadcrumbList structured data.
Three domains (pf-lawllc, famlegalatl, entlegalatl)
Family Law and Entertainment Law become sections of one site, one phone number, one brand.
Stale homepage clone and WooCommerce pages indexed
Old URLs redirect to their new homes; same practice-area URLs kept for rankings.
“Top-Rated”, “Best”, open-ended “LIMITED TIME”
Removed from firm-voice copy; results and reviews carry disclaimers.

Before launch.

  1. Firm’s counsel reviews the privacy policy, legal disclaimer, results wording and the bios.
  2. Attorney confirms FAQ wording on Georgia divorce grounds (“irretrievably broken”) and separate maintenance.
  3. Confirm office days and hours, and which job openings are current.
  4. Connect the consultation form to the firm’s intake system (Intaker or MyCase).
  5. Point famlegalatl.com and entlegalatl.com at the new division pages with 301 redirects.
  6. Remove the proposal banner and the noindex tag, then submit the sitemap in Search Console.

Built as a static Astro site. It can keep reading content from the firm’s existing WordPress, so staff edit where they already do.
