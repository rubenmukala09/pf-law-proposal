Do you have an estate plan? Insights from an Atlanta estate planning attorney

Why estate planning matters for your family, and the five key components of a plan: trusted advisors, wills and trusts, healthcare directives, insurance documentation, and financial powers of attorney.

By The Law Offices of Precious Felder, LLC

It’s critical to know whether you need a well-organized estate plan. Consulting an Atlanta estate planning attorney can protect your assets, honor your wishes, and give your entire family peace of mind. At The Law Offices of Precious Felder, LLC, we help families across Georgia create estate plans that are comprehensive, practical, and tailored to each family’s unique needs.

Why you need an Atlanta estate planning attorney

Many families delay estate planning conversations because they feel uncomfortable discussing finances, medical care, and end-of-life decisions. However, avoiding this discussion can lead to confusion, disputes, and unnecessary legal costs.

An experienced Atlanta estate planning attorney ensures your parents’ decisions are respected and legally protected. Proper planning allows families to:

  • Protect assets for future generations
  • Minimize probate delays and legal fees
  • Document medical wishes clearly
  • Maintain family harmony

The five key components of an estate plan

1. trusted advisors

Encourage your parents to list all trusted advisors, including attorneys, financial planners, accountants, and doctors. This ensures their appointed decision-makers can access the right professionals when necessary.

2. wills and trusts

A will or revocable living trust is the foundation of any estate plan. Confirm that your parents have:

  • A current will or trust
  • A named executor or trustee
  • Documents stored in a secure, accessible location

Even if you do not review these documents, knowing their location ensures your parents’ wishes are carried out effectively.

3. healthcare directives

A living will and medical power of attorney allow a trusted person to make healthcare decisions on behalf of your parents if they cannot do so. Encourage your parents to discuss their medical preferences with the appointed agent to ensure their wishes are fully understood.

4. insurance documentation

Maintaining records of all insurance policies (life, health, long-term care, disability, home, and auto) is essential. This ensures policies are accessible when decisions need to be made quickly.

5. financial accounts and powers of attorney

A financial power of attorney allows a trusted individual to manage checking, savings, investment, and retirement accounts in the event of incapacity. This ensures bills are paid, assets are protected, and day-to-day finances continue uninterrupted.

Benefits of working with an Atlanta estate planning attorney

Estate planning is more than drafting documents; it’s about securing your family’s future. By working with an Atlanta estate planning attorney, your parents can:

  • Protect their legacy and assets
  • Avoid probate complications
  • Ensure medical decisions reflect their wishes
  • Minimize family disputes

At The Law Offices of Precious Felder, LLC, we provide personalized, compassionate guidance to help families create estate plans that reflect their goals and values.

Next steps: Protect your family’s future

Start planning today to ensure your parents’ wishes are honored and their assets are secure. Schedule a consultation with your trusted Atlanta estate planning attorney to create a plan that protects your family and provides peace of mind.
