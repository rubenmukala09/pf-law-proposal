Since 2012

We show up ready.

An Atlanta firm for families and creatives, founded by Atlanta native Precious Felder.

Since 2012, The Law Offices of Precious Felder, LLC has served clients with a wide range of legal disputes. Our lawyers represent many kinds of clients, but clients with complex legal issues or high-conflict situations will benefit most from our firm.

Founded in 2012, the firm offers comprehensive legal representation in both family law and entertainment law. With a focus on the intricate dynamics of family matters and the complex landscape of entertainment industry regulations, our seasoned attorneys are committed to safeguarding your interests and pursuing favorable outcomes. Trust us to be your steadfast advocate, offering guidance and support every step of the way.

What we hold ourselves to.

  • Communication

    At the heart of our practice is a deep commitment to fostering strong, lasting relationships with both our clients and our team. We believe that open communication is the cornerstone of any successful partnership, and we make it a priority to engage with honesty, integrity, and transparency in all our interactions. Our core values are built on these principles, as we understand that trust is fundamental to achieving good outcomes in any legal matter.

  • Preparation

    When we say we show up ready, it means we’ve done our homework thoroughly and meticulously. Our preparation involves an in-depth analysis of the case, a comprehensive review of all relevant documents, and the formulation of a well-considered strategy to guide us forward. We recognize that success in the legal field hinges on meticulous preparation, and we are committed to leaving no stone unturned.

  • Teamwork

    We also firmly believe in the power of teamwork as a cornerstone of our practice. Collaborating closely with our clients, we craft tailored legal strategies designed to meet their specific needs and goals. Our dedicated team of experienced attorneys and skilled staff members is committed to delivering exceptional service and unwavering support.

  • Synergy

    We are firm believers in the power of synergy, understanding that when we work together as a cohesive team, we can accomplish far more than any of us could individually. Our approach is centered around creating an environment where each team member’s unique strengths and talents are fully utilized to serve our clients’ best interests.
