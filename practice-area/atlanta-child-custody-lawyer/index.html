Family Law · Child Custody & Support

Advocating for your children and your rights

We represent parents across metro Atlanta in original custody and child support matters and post-divorce modifications, working toward arrangements that serve your child’s best interests while protecting your role as a parent.

Overview

Few legal matters carry greater weight than decisions about the care of a child. Custody and support outcomes shape your daily life, your child’s stability, and your future relationship as a family. You deserve an attorney who brings both legal skill and steady judgment to these decisions, someone who will prepare thoroughly, communicate clearly, and advocate with conviction.

The Law Offices of Precious Felder, LLC represents parents across the metro area in both original custody matters and post-divorce modifications. We work to secure arrangements that serve your child’s best interests while protecting your role as a parent.

How Georgia courts determine custody

Under Georgia law, custody determinations are guided by the best interests of the child. Courts consider a broad range of factors, including each parent’s ability to provide for the child’s needs, the existing emotional bond between parent and child, the home environment, and any history of family violence or substance abuse. The controlling statutes appear in O.C.G.A. Title 19, Chapter 9.

Georgia distinguishes between legal custody (the authority to make major decisions about education, healthcare, religion, and extracurricular activities) and physical custody, which governs where the child resides. A thoughtful, court-approved Parenting Plan is required in every case, setting a clear schedule and decision-making framework. Additional guidance for families is available through the Judicial Council of Georgia.

How child support is calculated in Georgia

Child support in Georgia is determined using an Income Shares model, which considers the gross income of both parents, the number of children, the cost of health insurance and work-related childcare, and certain statutory adjustments. The calculation is performed using the official Georgia Child Support Commission worksheet. Ensuring that income is reported accurately and that all applicable deviations are properly presented is essential to a fair result.

Modifications and enforcement

Life circumstances change, and existing custody or support orders may need to be modified when a substantial change in conditions occurs, such as a job change, relocation, or a shift in the child’s needs. When a parent fails to comply with an existing order, enforcement remedies are available, including contempt proceedings and wage withholding. Our firm handles both modification and enforcement matters with the same attention to detail that guides our original custody work.

How we help

Our approach combines experienced advocacy with the sensitivity these matters require. We begin by understanding your family’s circumstances and your goals as a parent, then prepare each case with the thoroughness the court expects: complete financial documentation, a well-drafted Parenting Plan, and a clear, credible presentation of the facts. From first consultation through final order, you will have an attorney who is responsive, professional, and fully committed to protecting your relationship with your child.
