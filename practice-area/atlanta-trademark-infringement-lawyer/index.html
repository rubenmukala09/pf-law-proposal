Entertainment Law · Trademark Infringement

Defend your brand

When someone uses your brand without permission, we investigate, send cease-and-desist demands, coordinate platform takedowns, and pursue remedies under the Lanham Act and Georgia law, including federal litigation.

Overview

Attorney Precious Felder helps business owners, creatives, and brand builders stop counterfeits, knockoffs, and copycats, fast. When someone uses your brand without permission, every day of delay erodes customer trust, market share, and the goodwill you worked years to build. At The Law Offices of Precious Felder, LLC, our team moves quickly to investigate the infringement, issue aggressive cease-and-desist demands, and pursue every legal remedy available under the Lanham Act and Georgia law.

How we help

At The Law Offices of Precious Felder, LLC, we are dedicated to protecting your brand from unauthorized use and infringement. Our team works closely with you to assess the extent of the infringement, gather necessary evidence, and pursue effective legal remedies. We offer comprehensive support throughout the enforcement process, ensuring your brand’s rights are defended and your market position is secured.

Benefits of hiring a trademark infringement lawyer

As a proficient trademark law firm, we provide valuable assistance with:

  • Evidence Collection: We help gather and analyze evidence of trademark infringement to build a strong case and support your claims effectively.
  • Legal Action: Our attorneys are skilled in pursuing various legal actions, including cease-and-desist letters, negotiations, and litigation, to address and resolve trademark infringement issues.
  • Brand Protection: We work to secure favorable outcomes, such as damages or settlements, and implement strategies to prevent future infringements, safeguarding your brand’s reputation and value.
  • Cease-and-Desist & Platform Takedowns: Our team issues aggressive cease-and-desist letters and coordinates Amazon, Shopify, Etsy, and DMCA-style takedowns to remove infringing listings quickly.
  • Federal Litigation: When cease-and-desist is ignored, our team files suit in federal court under the Lanham Act to pursue injunctions, damages, and attorneys’ fees.

Remedies under the Lanham Act

Under the Lanham Act, a successful trademark infringement claim can recover actual damages, the infringer’s profits, and in willful or counterfeit cases, statutory damages up to $2 million per counterfeit mark. An experienced attorney also pursues injunctions that force the infringer to stop using your mark nationwide, destruction of counterfeit inventory, and attorneys’ fees in exceptional cases. For marks registered with the United States Patent and Trademark Office, you gain powerful federal enforcement rights that unregistered marks simply do not have.

Whether you are enforcing a federally registered trademark or a Georgia common law mark, our team builds a case designed to stop the bleeding and send a message to future copycats. From Amazon takedowns and DMCA-style platform enforcement to full-scale federal litigation, we tailor the response to the scale of the infringement and the value of your brand.
