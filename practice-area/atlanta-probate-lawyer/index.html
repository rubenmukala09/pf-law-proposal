Guiding families through probate

Overview

Attorney Precious Felder guides Georgia executors, administrators, and beneficiaries through the full probate process in Fulton, DeKalb, Cobb, and Gwinnett Probate Courts. Losing a loved one is already hard. Navigating will admission, creditor claims, asset marshalling, and Georgia inheritance law on your own makes it harder. Our boutique Atlanta firm carries that administrative load so your family can focus on grieving and healing.

Whether you are the named executor of a simple estate, an administrator of an intestate estate, a beneficiary worried about delays, or a family member contesting a will, we handle petitions, inventories, accountings, guardianships, and conservatorships from start to finish.

Georgia probate law explained, step by step

Probate is the legal process by which a deceased person’s estate is administered and distributed. In Georgia, this involves several key steps:

  • Understanding Probate Laws: Georgia’s probate laws govern the distribution of assets, payment of debts, and settlement of the estate. The process begins by filing the deceased’s will (if one exists) with the probate court.
  • Executor Duties: The executor or personal representative appointed in the will (or by the court if there is no will) is responsible for managing the estate. Duties include gathering and valuing assets, paying debts and taxes, and distributing the remaining assets according to the will or state law.
  • Inheritance Distribution: Assets are distributed according to the terms of the will or, if there is no will, according to Georgia’s intestacy laws. This process must be handled with care to ensure compliance with legal requirements and the decedent’s wishes.

Probate can involve various issues such as will disputes, guardianship, and conservatorship, each requiring careful legal attention to resolve effectively.

Our probate services

Handling probate involves multiple stages, including understanding probate laws, executor duties, and inheritance distribution. Our comprehensive services ensure that all legal requirements are met efficiently and respectfully. We help minimize probate fees and streamline the probate process, ensuring your loved one’s wishes are honored and family disputes are handled with sensitivity.

Our services include:

  • Will Disputes: Addressing and resolving disagreements over the validity or terms of a will.
  • Guardianship/Conservatorship: Establishing legal guardianships or conservatorships for individuals who are unable to manage their own affairs.
  • Administration of Estate: Overseeing the entire process of settling the estate, from asset management to debt payment and distribution.
  • Probate Wills: Handling the probate of wills, ensuring that all terms are executed in accordance with the law and the decedent’s wishes.

How we manage the estate

Our team begins with a probate roadmap. We help you decide between solemn form versus common form probate, file the petition with the appropriate Georgia Probate Court, secure letters testamentary or letters of administration, and publish required notices to creditors. From there we build an inventory, marshal real estate, bank accounts, investments, and business interests, and coordinate appraisals where the estate warrants it.

Complex estates often involve more than paperwork. We handle creditor negotiations, resolve title and survivorship questions on real property, manage ongoing rental income, file final federal and state fiduciary income tax returns in coordination with IRS estate tax rules, and defend or prosecute will contests, undue influence claims, and breach of fiduciary duty cases when disputes arise. We also petition for guardianship and conservatorship where minors or incapacitated adults are involved.
