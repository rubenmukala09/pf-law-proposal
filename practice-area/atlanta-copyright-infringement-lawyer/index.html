Entertainment Law · Copyright Infringement

Enforce your rights

We act for creators, labels, studios, and brands whose work has been copied, reposted, sampled, or commercialized without permission, from DMCA takedowns and cease-and-desist letters to federal lawsuits.

Overview

Attorney Precious Felder defends creators, labels, studios, and brands whose copyrighted work has been stolen, reposted, sampled, or commercialized without permission. Copyright law under Title 17 of the U.S. Code gives authors powerful federal remedies, but only when you enforce them decisively and on the right timeline.

From DMCA takedown notices and cease-and-desist letters to federal infringement lawsuits seeking injunctions, actual damages, and statutory damages up to $150,000 per work, our boutique Atlanta firm pursues every available remedy.

How we help

At The Law Offices of Precious Felder, LLC, we understand the critical nature of protecting your creative works from infringement. Our team works diligently to identify, address, and resolve copyright disputes, ensuring that your rights are upheld and your interests are safeguarded. We offer comprehensive support throughout the litigation process, providing you with the confidence and expertise needed to handle complex copyright infringement issues.

Benefits of hiring a copyright infringement lawyer

As a dedicated copyright law firm, we offer valuable assistance with:

  • Evidence Collection: We help gather and analyze evidence to build a strong case for copyright infringement, ensuring that you have the necessary documentation to support your claims.
  • Legal Action: Our attorneys are skilled in pursuing legal action against infringers, whether through negotiations, cease-and-desist letters, or litigation, to protect your intellectual property.
  • Resolution Strategies: We work to secure favorable outcomes, including damages or settlements, and take steps to prevent future infringements, protecting your creative works and financial interests.

How we enforce your rights

Our team starts with a focused investigation: we document the infringing use, trace it back to the responsible parties, and confirm that your work is registered, or expedite registration through the U.S. Copyright Office so you can sue. From there we escalate strategically, starting with DMCA takedowns to platforms like YouTube, Spotify, Instagram, and Amazon, then moving to cease-and-desist letters demanding removal, attribution, or payment.

When informal steps fail, we file federal lawsuits under the Copyright Act of 1976, pursuing injunctions, actual damages, infringer profits, and statutory damages that can reach $150,000 per willfully infringed work. We also defend creators who have been wrongly accused and negotiate licensing settlements that turn disputes into revenue. Whether your stolen work is a song, film, book, photograph, software, or brand asset, we build a case designed to end the infringement and recover what you are owed.

Related matters.

  • Copyright Registration

    We prepare and file applications with the U.S. Copyright Office for musicians, filmmakers, authors, and designers, because registration is required before you can sue for infringement or claim statutory damages.

  • Copyright & Trademarks

    We handle copyright registration, trademark clearance and USPTO filings, DMCA takedowns, cease-and-desist letters, licensing, and federal infringement litigation for creators, entrepreneurs, and entertainment companies.

  • Trademark Infringement

    When someone uses your brand without permission, we investigate, send cease-and-desist demands, coordinate platform takedowns, and pursue remedies under the Lanham Act and Georgia law, including federal litigation.
