Entertainment Law · Trademark Registration

Protect your brand

We take brand names and logos through federal registration with the USPTO, from clearance searches and intent-to-use applications to office action responses and post-registration maintenance.

Book a consultationCall (404) 355-9400

Overview

Attorney Precious Felder helps Georgia businesses, entrepreneurs, artists, and entertainment brands secure federal trademark protection through the United States Patent and Trademark Office. A registered mark gives your company nationwide priority, statutory damages against infringers, and the coveted ® symbol, all critical assets as your brand grows.

From clearance searches and intent-to-use applications to office action responses and post-registration maintenance, our boutique Atlanta firm handles every step of the trademark process.

How we help you register your mark

At The Law Offices of Precious Felder, LLC, we are committed to helping you navigate the complexities of trademark registration. Our team works closely with you to ensure that your trademark application is accurately prepared and submitted, addressing any potential issues before they arise. We provide strategic advice and support to secure your brand’s protection and enhance your market position.

Benefits of hiring a trademark registration lawyer

As a skilled trademark law firm, we offer valuable assistance with:

  • Trademark Search and Analysis: We conduct thorough searches to ensure your proposed trademark is unique and not in conflict with existing marks, reducing the risk of application rejection.
  • Application Preparation: Our attorneys prepare and file your trademark application, ensuring compliance with all legal requirements and minimizing the likelihood of delays or issues during the registration process.
  • Brand Protection: Once registered, we help you monitor and enforce your trademark rights, taking action against potential infringements to safeguard your brand’s integrity and value.

Key services

Our team starts every engagement with a comprehensive clearance search through the United States Patent and Trademark Office database, common-law sources, and state registries so you avoid filing a mark that is already taken or confusingly similar. Once the mark is cleared, we prepare and file the application, select the right classes of goods and services, and craft a specimen of use that the USPTO will accept on the first pass when possible.

We also respond to office actions, handle opposition and cancellation proceedings before the Trademark Trial and Appeal Board, and enforce your rights under the federal Lanham Act. Whether you are registering your first brand, expanding into new product categories, or protecting an existing trademark from infringement, we deliver clear, strategic guidance tailored to Atlanta entrepreneurs.
