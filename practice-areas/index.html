Practice areas.

Two divisions, twenty kinds of matters.

Family Law

Division overview

  • Divorce

    We represent individuals and families in contested and uncontested divorces across Fulton, DeKalb, Gwinnett, Cobb, and surrounding counties, explaining each step in plain language and building a strategy around your goals.

  • Child Custody & Support

    We represent parents across metro Atlanta in original custody and child support matters and post-divorce modifications, working toward arrangements that serve your child’s best interests while protecting your role as a parent.

  • Legitimation & Fathers’ Rights

    We help unmarried fathers throughout metro Atlanta legitimate their children, secure custody and visitation arrangements, and protect their parental role, including in contested legitimation hearings.

  • Custody Modification

    We help Georgia parents modify outdated or unworkable custody and visitation orders, handling the petition, discovery, guardian ad litem interviews, mediation, and final hearing, with a focus on your child’s stability.

  • Contempt of Court

    When a former spouse or co-parent ignores a Georgia family court order, we move to restore compliance. If you are the one accused, we build a fact-driven defense that protects your freedom, finances, and parental rights.

  • Wills & Trusts

    We draft wills and pour-over wills and create and fund revocable, irrevocable, special needs, and testamentary trusts tailored to your family, so your property is distributed as you intend.

  • Probate

    We guide executors, administrators, and beneficiaries through probate in Fulton, DeKalb, Cobb, and Gwinnett Probate Courts, from petitions, inventories, and accountings to will disputes, guardianships, and conservatorships.

  • Estate Planning

    We coordinate wills, trusts, durable powers of attorney, advance healthcare directives, and beneficiary designations into one plan for young professionals, parents, blended families, business owners, and retirees.

  • Nuptial Agreements

    We draft and negotiate prenuptial and postnuptial agreements for clients who own a business, have significant savings, expect an inheritance, or are entering a second marriage, with the full financial disclosure Georgia law requires.

Entertainment Law

Division overview

  • Music Industry

    We represent artists, labels, producers, and songwriters in recording and publishing deals, master and composition rights, trademarks, and royalty disputes, from a first contract to an independent label launch.

  • Television & Film

    We represent producers, directors, writers, actors, and production companies, drafting option, talent, location, chain-of-title, and distribution agreements and resolving on-set, guild, and union disputes.

  • Copyright & Trademarks

    We handle copyright registration, trademark clearance and USPTO filings, DMCA takedowns, cease-and-desist letters, licensing, and federal infringement litigation for creators, entrepreneurs, and entertainment companies.

  • Copyright Registration

    We prepare and file applications with the U.S. Copyright Office for musicians, filmmakers, authors, and designers, because registration is required before you can sue for infringement or claim statutory damages.

  • Copyright Infringement

    We act for creators, labels, studios, and brands whose work has been copied, reposted, sampled, or commercialized without permission, from DMCA takedowns and cease-and-desist letters to federal lawsuits.

  • Trademark Registration

    We take brand names and logos through federal registration with the USPTO, from clearance searches and intent-to-use applications to office action responses and post-registration maintenance.

  • Trademark Infringement

    When someone uses your brand without permission, we investigate, send cease-and-desist demands, coordinate platform takedowns, and pursue remedies under the Lanham Act and Georgia law, including federal litigation.

  • Business Formation

    We help entrepreneurs, creatives, and small business owners choose an entity, prepare formation documents and operating or shareholder agreements, and complete Georgia Secretary of State and EIN filings.

  • Right of Publicity

    We help musicians, actors, athletes, influencers, and executives stop unauthorized commercial use of their name, image, voice, or likeness, and negotiate licensing, endorsement, and NIL agreements.

  • Contracts

    We draft, review, and negotiate the agreements that shape music careers, including record, publishing, management, producer, distribution, licensing, and merchandising deals, for artists, labels, producers, managers, and songwriters.

  • Defamation

    We represent professionals, business owners, creatives, and private individuals harmed by false statements online, in the press, or in their industry, pursuing retractions, content removal, and damages under Georgia law.
