The team behind the attorneys.

Your first call, your scheduling and your paperwork run through them.

  • LaTesha Roberts

    Office Manager

    Office manager overseeing human resources, accounts, office administration, and marketing, after earlier roles as the firm’s legal secretary and intake and client relations manager.

    Read bio

    LaTesha Roberts, originally from Houston, Texas, moved to Georgia in 2020. LaTesha has prior professional legal experience, including office management and law office administration. She obtained her Associate Degree in Applied Science and Business Management and has also pursued paralegal studies. She joined our team as a Legal Secretary to assist the firm with administrative practice management, scheduling, file organization, and maintenance.

    After a year with the firm, she had become one of the clients’ favorite liaisons and was promoted to Intake and Client Relations Manager. After proving her importance by contributing to the firm’s significant growth, she transitioned to become the firm’s Office Manager. Ms. Roberts oversees human resources, accounts receivable, accounts payable, office administration, and marketing.

    When she is not assisting the firm, she is a busy full-time mom who enjoys bowling and watching horror movies.

  • Karine Cesar

    Client Relations Specialist

    Client relations specialist with a background in legal intake and sales, fluent in French and Haitian Creole.

    Read bio

    Karine Cesar brings a unique blend of cultural insight, academic prowess, and professional experience to her role at the firm. Born in Haiti, Karine’s journey began with her parents’ pursuit of a better life, leading them to settle briefly in Queens, NY, before finding their permanent home in Irvington, NJ, where Karine was raised alongside her younger sisters. Driven by a thirst for knowledge and a passion for personal development, Karine pursued a diverse educational path. She studied Sociology and Psychology at Rutgers New Brunswick, honed her business acumen through classes at Pace University, and furthered her legal knowledge with paralegal courses at Broward County College in South Florida.

    In 2005, Karine’s family made the decision to relocate to Georgia, solidifying their commitment to building a brighter future together. With a wealth of professional experience under her belt, including roles in mortgage industry call centers, T-1 sales, and legal intake sales, Karine is well-equipped to navigate the intricacies of the sales landscape. Outside of her professional pursuits, Karine finds joy in a diverse range of interests. An avid enthusiast of British crime programs and the ID Channel, she also indulges her creative side through activities such as diamond painting and picture finds.

    Karine also dedicates her time to serving at church and nurturing her garden, finding solace and fulfillment in giving back to her community and connecting with nature. Fluent in both French and Haitian Creole, Karine’s multicultural background enriches her interactions with clients and colleagues alike, allowing her to bridge cultural divides and foster meaningful connections. With a commitment to excellence, a passion for continuous learning, and a genuine desire to make a positive impact, Karine Cesar is dedicated to delivering attentive service and exceeding the expectations of her clients.

  • Crystal Thompson

    Paralegal

    Paralegal who drafts briefs, memoranda, motions, and correspondence and supports clients through case management and discovery.

    Read bio

    Crystal is a detail-oriented paralegal who applies excellent writing skills in drafting briefs, legal memorandums, motions, and correspondence. She conducts all legal business professionally and with precision and minimal back-and-forth.

    Crystal is an extremely organized paralegal who can manage caseloads with authority and advanced skill. She is capable of collaborating with a diverse range of legal personnel, including associates, partners, and legal secretaries. Crystal regularly assists clients with case management and the completion of discovery. She is an integral part of the litigation process and communicates with clients regularly.

    Crystal attended Valdosta State University, earning a degree in Political Science. She then attended the University of Georgia, where she received her Paralegal Certificate.

  • Zakia Dora

    Legal Assistant

    Legal assistant and U.S. Navy retiree who supports attorneys and clients across family and entertainment law matters with intake, scheduling, and document preparation.

    Read bio

    Zakia Dora is a Covington, Georgia native who brings dedication, discipline, and a strong commitment to service to her role as Legal Assistant. Following her retirement from the U.S. Navy, Zakia pursued her Bachelor of Arts in English at the University of West Georgia, where she was inspired by mentors and peers to deepen her engagement in the community. She has been an active member of several service and civic organizations, including Rotary International, Rotaract of West Georgia, Carroll County CASA, The Child Advocacy Center, and the Junior League of Dallas.

    Her path to the legal profession was driven by both her long-standing interest in law and her passion for helping people navigate important moments in their lives. Zakia plays a vital role in supporting attorneys and clients across family and entertainment law matters. She assists with intake, scheduling, case organization, document preparation, and ongoing communication, ensuring that every client interaction reflects professionalism, care, and attention to detail.

    Clients and colleagues alike value Zakia’s approachable nature and her ability to help create a smooth, supportive experience from the first call to the resolution of a matter. Her diverse background in military service, higher education, and community involvement provides a strong foundation for the high level of service she delivers to clients.

    Outside the office, Zakia enjoys spending time with her family, reading, gardening, traveling, and playing tennis.
