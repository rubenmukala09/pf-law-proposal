About Sivonnia

Sivonnia DeBarros is a seasoned attorney with more than a decade of litigation, intellectual property, and business law experience across state and federal courts. Her practice focuses on protecting brands, resolving complex disputes, and providing strategic legal guidance to entrepreneurs, companies, and creatives. Throughout her career, she has handled federal trademark litigation, contract negotiations, employment discrimination matters, ADA website and data privacy disputes, and a broad range of commercial conflicts.

Sivonnia’s litigation background is rooted in hands-on courtroom experience. She previously served as a 711 Law Clerk with the Illinois Attorney General’s Office and the Cook County State’s Attorney’s Office, where she appeared in court, conducted legal research, drafted substantive motions, including summary judgment motions and motions to dismiss, and supported attorneys handling constitutional, criminal, and civil matters. Her early work also includes consumer bankruptcy roles assisting individuals through high-stakes financial transitions.

Her legal acumen extends beyond litigation. Sivonnia has drafted and negotiated a wide array of commercial agreements, including licensing deals, content contracts, NDAs, publishing agreements, and international service arrangements, and has advised companies on business structures, operational strategy, and cross-industry coordination. She has successfully resolved matters involving statewide and federal regulatory issues, ADA compliance, recyclability claims, and data privacy disputes.

Sivonnia is admitted to practice in Illinois, Wisconsin, and Georgia, as well as in multiple federal courts. She is an accomplished author, a former Division I track and field athlete, and an active legal leader currently serving as Chair of the Illinois State Bar Association’s Sports & Entertainment Law Section. Her diverse experience, strategic mindset, and commitment to empowering clients make her a trusted advocate and a powerful addition to our team.
